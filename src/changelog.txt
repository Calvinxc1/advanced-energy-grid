Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026.08.24
  Compatibility:
    - Made Space Age an optional dependency, so the mod now loads on base Factorio 2.1.
  Changes:
    - Restricted the big electric pole MK4 and substation MK4 tiers to Space Age, since their recipes need superconductor and quantum processor.
    - Restricted the huge electric pole MK3 and MK4 tiers to Space Age, since their recipes need superconductor, foundation, and quantum processor.
    - Kept every other tier, including medium electric pole MK4, available without Space Age.
    - Gated research that required electromagnetic or cryogenic science on space science instead when Space Age is absent.
    - Stopped registering the Space Age pole tiers with Power Overload when Space Age is absent.
    - Split the crafting menu into a local distribution row holding small poles, medium poles and substations, and a distance transmission row holding big poles and huge poles. Every tier of a family now sits in one row in tier order, starting from its vanilla tier, rather than sharing a single row with every other pole type and with pipes.
